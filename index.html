Where do the numbers come from?

Figures are published area values. The NCR multiples are calculated by dividing each place by 619.54 km².

Fact checked
🏝️ Boracay Island — 10.32 km²DENR-EMB Region VI, Boracay Island Annual Assessment Report 2022, Section 1 · Philippine Journal of Science / DOST research article, opening description
🌴 Siargao Island — about 437 km²International Labour Organization, Tourism Sector in Siargao, page 1 · NGCP Customer Bulletin 2025-029, attached project material
🏙️ NCR — 619.54 km²Philippine Statistics Authority, 2020 census population-density highlights · DENR-NCR, 2023 Regional State of the Environment report
🦁 Singapore — 745.1 km² (June 2026)Singapore Department of Statistics, Environment data tables · Singapore Government Data Portal, Land Area (As At June)
🚢 Hong Kong SAR — 1,114.57 km²Hong Kong Yearbook 2024, page 1, Land Area · HK Connect, Facts and Figures
🌋 Bali Province — 5,582.83 km²Statistics Indonesia (BPS), 2025 Bali area table · Bali Provincial Government, “Sekilas Bali” (notes differing published figures)
🐉 Taiwan territory — 36,197.3371 km²Taiwan Executive Yuan, land profile · Taiwan Ministry of the Interior, Demographic Fact Book 2024, Table 10

Geography note: Boracay and Siargao are islands; Bali is a province; NCR, Singapore and Hong Kong are administrative areas; Taiwan uses its official territory-area figure including outlying islands. Bali official sources publish conflicting figures, so this game uses the newer Statistics Indonesia table for consistency. Siargao's figure is approximate.
